# Business Research & Idea Selection

Date: 2026-07-14

## Candidates evaluated

| Criterion | DailyTools.in | DevUtilsHub.com | SmartBusinessKit.com |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search demand | Very high — "gst calculator", "emi calculator", "word counter", "compress image" are evergreen, million-plus monthly queries in aggregate | High but concentrated on head terms (json formatter, regex tester) | Moderate — invoice/business templates, narrower query universe |
| Competition | High on head terms (bank/fintech brands), weak on long-tail variants | Very high (many established dev-tool sites) | Moderate |
| SEO difficulty | Medium — long-tail + programmatic pages are winnable | High — entrenched incumbents with backlinks | Medium |
| Monetization (AdSense RPM) | Strong — finance content earns $15–50 RPM vs $5–20 general | **Weak — ~75% of developers use ad blockers** | Strong RPM but lower volume |
| Repeat usage | High (calculators, counters, converters are habitual) | High | Medium |
| Future potential | Scales to 100+ tools across many niches | Capped by audience | Capped by niche size |
| AdSense approval fit | Good with substantial original content per tool | Good | Good |

## Decision

**Build the DailyTools concept** — an everyday-utilities hub with a
**finance-calculator core** — under original branding **ToolNow**.

Rationale:
1. Finance calculator visitors are the highest-RPM, least ad-blocked
   audience reachable with zero-budget SEO.
2. General utilities (text, files, generators) add breadth, repeat
   visits, and internal-linking surface without diluting the finance core.
3. Developer tools are still included as a category (they cost nothing
   to run client-side) but are not the monetization bet.
4. The model scales linearly: every new tool is a static page with its
   own keyword cluster — no marginal infrastructure cost.

## Branding

"ToolNow" — descriptive, memorable, low trademark risk. Tagline:
*"Free online tools that just work."* Original logo (tile + "T" + spark),
original teal/amber design system, original copy throughout. No UI,
code, text or branding was copied from any competitor; competitor sites
were reviewed only for market comparison, not design or content.
